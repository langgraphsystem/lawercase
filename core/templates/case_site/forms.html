Tips for Filling PDF Forms

  • Fill directly in browser: Modern browsers (Chrome, Edge, Firefox) allow you to fill PDF forms directly. Click on fields and type.
  • Save your progress: Use "Download" or Ctrl+S to save the filled PDF to your computer.
  • Print when ready: After filling, print the form and sign where required before mailing to USCIS.
  • Use Adobe Reader: For best experience with complex forms, download Adobe Acrobat Reader (free).
  • Keep copies: Always save a copy of all filled forms for your records before submission.
